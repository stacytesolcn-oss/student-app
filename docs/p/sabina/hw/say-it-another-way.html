Say it another way

The phrases from our travel lesson. Write each sentence again with the phrase. Keep the meaning the same.

How to do it
  1. Read the sentence.
  2. Write it again with the phrase. You can change the form: try → tried, my → her.
  3. Tap Check. Stuck? Tap Hint first.
  4. At the end, tap Copy my answers and send them to me in Telegram.

Example: I'm excited about the weekend. → I'm looking forward to the weekend.
